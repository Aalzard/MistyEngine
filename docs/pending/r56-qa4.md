## Fuzzing

A temporary harness (not committed) loaded every SampleProject `.scene` / `.prefab` under
300 KB with random value mutations (huge and negative numbers, 2^32 − 1, 2^64 − 1, wrong
types, nulls, empty arrays and objects) and truncations, then cloned the scene, built physics
and stepped physics and particles. First pass (600 rounds per file): 15 panics, all in rapier /
parry from absurd scales or rotations (F2, PH4). After those fixes, 800 rounds per file (about
70 000 loads): 2 panics, both Floaters (PH5). No hangs (10 s limit per load) and no panics in
the loaders themselves.
