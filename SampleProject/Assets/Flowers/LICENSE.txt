Meadow Wildflowers - Misty Engine sample assets

The files in this folder (the flower textures) are original works made with Misty Engine's
own tools (tools/flower_textures.py, a procedural generator). They contain no photographs and
no third-party or downloaded content.

They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You can copy, modify and use them in your own games and projects, commercial or not,
without asking and without crediting anyone (a credit is welcome but not required).
