# Meadow Trees (Misty Engine sample assets)

Five broadleaf trees for terrains, made entirely by Misty Engine's own tools: the trees are
procedural recipes (`.tree` files) the engine turns into meshes, and every texture was
generated by `tools/tree_textures.py` (no photos, no third-party or downloaded assets).
They are used by the `MeadowDemo` scene and are free to use in your own games (see
`LICENSE.txt`).

| Asset | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Oak.tree` | A meadow oak, about 14 m: short sturdy trunk dividing into big limbs, a wide rounded crown of lobed leaves. |
| `Oak Broad.tree` | An old open-grown oak, about 17 m, broad and spreading. |
| `Young Oak.tree` | A young oak, about 7.5 m, an oval crown on a slim trunk. |
| `Linden.tree` | A lime (linden), about 17 m: straight trunk, tall dome of heart-shaped leaves. |
| `Birch.tree` | A silver birch, about 15 m: slim white trunk to the top, a light airy crown. |

`Textures/` holds, per species (Oak, Linden, Birch):

| Texture | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `<Species>Bark.png` | Bark albedo, 1024 × 1024, tileable both ways (`u` around the trunk, image up = up the trunk). |
| `<Species>BarkNormal.png` | Tangent-space normal map (OpenGL: green up). |
| `<Species>BarkRoughness.png` | Roughness (grayscale, 1 = rough). |
| `<Species>BarkAO.png` | Ambient occlusion of the furrows (grayscale). |
| `<Species>BarkHeight.png` | Height (grayscale, 0 = deepest furrow; parallax). |
| `<Species>Leaves.png` | Leaf atlas, 1024 × 1024: 2 × 2 leafy twigs, the twig entering at the bottom centre of each cell, alpha = cut-out. |
| `<Species>LeavesNormal.png` | The leaves' normal map. |
| `<Species>LeavesRoughness.png` | Roughness (upper sides a little waxy, undersides matte). |
| `<Species>LeavesTranslucency.png` | How much sunlight each part lets through (thin blades glow, veins and twigs less). |

The roughness, occlusion, height and translucency slots of a `.tree` are optional; empty slots use
plain values.

## Using them

1. Select a Terrain, open the **Paint Trees** tab (🌳) in the Inspector.
2. **+ Add Tree** and pick one of these `.tree` assets (add several for a mixed wood).
3. Left click in the Scene view to plant trees with the brush (Shift + click removes them),
   or **Mass Place Trees** to scatter them over the whole terrain.
4. **Tree Rendering** sets how far trees are drawn and where they switch to lighter meshes
   and billboards.

Trees sway with the terrain's wind (Grass & Details ▸ Wind) and the scene's Wind Zone, cast
shadows, and have trunk colliders when the terrain has a Terrain Collider.

To make your own tree, select one of these and change it in the Inspector (seed, height,
crown shape and width, limbs, leaf density and size, tints, textures, wind), or use
**Assets ▸ Create ▸ Tree**. Duplicate an asset first if you want to keep the original.

## Making the textures again

    python tools/tree_textures.py SampleProject/Assets/Trees/Textures

(NumPy, SciPy and Pillow; deterministic, `--preview` also writes review sheets). The script documents its layouts and
conventions.
