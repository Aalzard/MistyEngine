# Rocks

Six procedurally generated rocks for natural scenes, made for the MeadowDemo meadow and as
**Misty Virtual Geometry** showcase material:

| Prefab | Stone | Size | Dense mesh | LOD 1 / 2 / 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boulder Large | weathered granite, rounded, peeling sheets | ~5 m | 82 k triangles | 15 % / 4 % / 1 % |
| Boulder Split | granite block, joint faces, a deep split | ~3.4 m | 82 k | 15 % / 4 % / 1 % |
| Boulder Round | granite, egg-shaped, mossy top | ~2.3 m | 82 k | 15 % / 4 % / 1 % |
| Outcrop Layered | sandstone slab, stepped layers | ~8 m | 82 k | 15 % / 4 % / 1 % |
| Rock Small | granite fragment | ~0.8 m | 20 k | 20 % / 6 % / 2 % |
| Cobbles | seven river cobbles | ~1 m | 36 k | 20 % / 6 % / 2 % |

Each rock is:

- `Meshes/<Name>.glb`: the dense mesh, imported with **Virtual Geometry** on (clusters
  with per-cluster level of detail when the Quality level has Virtual Geometry on);
- `Meshes/<Name> LODs.glb`: three simplified levels made by the same simplifier;
- `Textures/<Name>_Albedo.jpg`, `_Normal.png` (OpenGL), `_Mask.png` (G = occlusion,
  A = smoothness): its own maps in its own UV layout, baked from tileable granite, sandstone,
  cobble, moss and lichen sets: lichen on exposed faces, moss on top faces and in hollows,
  a damp, grass-tinted band where the rock meets the ground;
- `Materials/<Name>.mat`: Standard; the colour is the rock's overall colour (Misty GI
  bounces it), the albedo map holds the detail;
- `Prefabs/<Name>.prefab`: a LOD Group over LOD 0 (the dense mesh) and LOD 1–3, a Mesh
  Collider on LOD 2 (not on Cobbles). With Virtual Geometry on, LOD 0 draws at every
  distance and the other levels are skipped.

The origin of every rock is on its ground line: place it on the terrain and the part below
(about a quarter of its height) is buried. Scale uniformly (0.8–1.3 looks natural); turn it
about its up axis freely; tilt it a little towards the slope.

## Regenerating

    python tools/rock_textures.py target/rock-textures
    cargo test --release -p misty_editor -- --ignored write_rocks

`crates/misty_editor/src/sample_rocks` holds the shapes, the bake and the prefabs;
`tools/rock_textures.py` the tileable material sets (NumPy, SciPy, Pillow).
